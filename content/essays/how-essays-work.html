Adding an essay

An essay is two things: one entry in site.json and one file in content/essays/. The entry holds the title, the date, a one-line summary and a slug. The file is named after the slug and holds the text.

The text is plain HTML. A paragraph goes in a <p>, a section heading in an <h2>, and a line you want to stand out in a <blockquote>.
